## Verification

Every row's source URL was opened and quoted on 2026-10-22.

**Row 1 — Lilly Endowment → MACC ($6,290,000)**
- https://maccsa.org/en/rural-ministry-initiative (MACC Rural Ministry Initiative page, live 2026-10-22) — title: "Rural Ministry Initiative — MACC is honored to lead the *Caminando Juntos: Accompanying Small-Town and Rural Pastoral Leaders Project*… generously funded by the Lilly Endowment." Press release, dated "December 31, 2024": "Mexican American Catholic College Receives $6,290,000 Grant for Ministry in Rural Areas and Small Towns — San Antonio, TX – Mexican American Catholic College (MACC) has received a grant of $6,290,000 from Lilly Endowment Inc. through its Ministry in Rural Areas and Small Towns Initiative." The page lists monthly project newsletters incl. "[December 2025 Newsletter- Archdiocese of San Antonio]".
- https://insidecharity.org/2025/06/28/mexican-american-catholic-college-celebrates-san-antonio-nonprofits (live 2026-10-22) — "Mexican American Catholic College Celebrates San Antonio Nonprofits," June 28, 2025: "Mexican American Catholic College was founded in 1972 by the Archdiocese of San Antonio and the Texas Catholic Conference as the Mexican American Cultural Center for pastoral formation and language study."
- Caveat: award was announced Dec. 31, 2024 (one day before the 2025-01-01 announcement window) and was captured here because it was publicly *reported* in-window (June 2025). MACC is an independent institution, not a canonically owned ArchSA entity; row included for completeness, flagged.

**Row 2 — Kenedy Memorial Foundation → ArchSA entities ($620,000, 10 grants, 2025)**
- https://www.archsa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Grant-Strategy-Stewardship_Learning-the-Basics-of-Grants_Cynthia-Hoffman.pdf (live 2026-10-22) — deck by "Cynthia Hoffman, Grants Manager, Archdiocese of SA," hosted on archsa.org (upload path 2026/09). Verbatim: "Catholic Focused Funders The John G. & Marie Stella Kenedy Memorial Foundation… Please note that this is a competitive grant and includes all Catholic entities within the Archdiocese of San Antonio. In 2025, we received 32 requests totaling $1.6 million dollars. We were only allocated $620,000 and it was divided between 10 grants. • If you are a K-12 Catholic school, please do NOT apply for tuition assistance. The Archdiocese submits a grant request for Hope for the Future funding."
- Cross-checked https://kenedy.org/grants (live 2026-10-22): "The charts below represent grant funding as of 12/31/2025. Click on each image to see the details" — aggregate charts only, no named recipients public.

**Row 3 — Catholic Community Foundation → Catholic Charities ArchSA ($125,000+, 2025)**
- https://ccftx.org/annual-reports/annual-report-2024 (live 2026-10-22) — "Annual Report 2024 … 2024 THE YEAR IN REVIEW"; "Sustaining Hope Through Faith and Giving" section verbatim: "Through the generosity of donors and the stewardship of eight endowments benefiting Catholic Charities programs, the **Catholic Community Foundation (CCF)** awarded more than **$125,000 in grants in 2025** to support the organization's mission of serving those in need. Over the past six years, Catholic Charities has received a total of **$370,000 in grants** from these endowments…" and "Endowment funding has also helped Catholic Charities grow its counseling services… In 2025, this support even covered unexpected costs, such as the replacement of an air conditioning system…" Page's site assets uploaded 2026-01, i.e., the 2025 distributions were publicly reported in 2026.

### Years covered with no findings / gaps

- **2027**: no grant awards announced/reported to date for any ArchSA entity (expected: Kenedy 2026-cycle funding disbursed Sept/Oct 2027 per the Hoffman deck; MACC Caminando Juntos subgrant cycles ongoing).
- **2025–2026 named-grant coverage gap**: archdiocesan and local reporting names no individual parish/school grant awards from external funders in the window. Kenedy's public grants page shows only aggregate charts; Koch Foundation, Raskob Foundation, OCP parish grants, the Community Foundation of the Texas Hill Country, and Hal & Charlie Peterson Foundation (both named in the archdiocese's own "Grants & Giving" deck as funder fits for Hill Country parishes, particularly for 2025 flood-recovery) have no public 2025–2027 grantee lists naming ArchSA entities that were found.
- **Todayscatholic.org limitation**: todayscatholic.org is the newspaper of the Diocese of Fort Wayne–South Bend, not San Antonio. San Antonio's Today's Catholic (est. 1892) now publishes at archsa.org/todays-catholic; its content was searched via Tavily `site:archsa.org` queries ("grant," "awarded," "Kenedy," "Catholic Community Foundation," "received a grant") — no 2025–2027 articles naming external grant awards to specific ArchSA parishes/schools beyond Row 5.
- **Facebook-sourced item excluded**: a Facebook post from a "St. Francis de Paula" parish reported a $25,000 Wyatt Ranches Foundation grant for church foundation repairs, but the post is not publicly retrievable ("This content isn't available right now" when opened 2026-10-22) and the identifiably located St. Francis de Paula parish (San Diego, TX) belongs to the Diocese of Corpus Christi — excluded per the live-URL sourcing rule.
- **Texas Veterans Commission Fund for Veterans Assistance 2025–26 awards list** (tvc.texas.gov PDF) was checked for Catholic Charities, Archdiocese of San Antonio — not a listed recipient (only Catholic Charities of Central Texas, Austin diocese).
- **Catholic Community Foundation FY2025 annual report** not yet published (the FY2024 report, published 2026, carries the 2025 distributions cited in Row 3). CCF's 2024 DAF-grant-recipient list and 2024 endowment distributions ($2,940,000, 57 grants) predate the 2025-01-01 window and are therefore not rowed.
- **San Fernando Catholic Foundation**: no 2025–2027 grantee announcements found.
- **"Today's Tomorrow" campaign reports** were considered and set aside: they report philanthropic *gifts received* by the archdiocese, not grants awarded.
