**What it funds.** A national Catholic foundation (Gainesville, FL) whose five stated funding priorities are Direct Evangelization, Mass Media, Preparation of Evangelists, Capital Expenditures, and Catholic Schools. Funding is for one year only; priority goes to "financially-distressed, underdeveloped areas"; endowments, capital campaigns, vehicles, land, and debt are excluded, and "discernment programs, minor seminaries, and individual grants are not eligible." **TEXAS-ELIGIBLE:** the foundation is national, and the Archdiocese of San Antonio's own grants-training deck (archsa.org, 2025) explicitly lists the Koch Foundation as an approved "Catholic friendly funder" for archSA parishes — noting it "Requires a letter of support from Archbishop Gustavo."

**Why we qualify (partially).** Eligibility names Catholic non-profits recognized by the Holy See; dioceses may submit up to six requests per year, and an archdiocesan faith-formation program could be framed as Preparation of Evangelists / Direct Evangelization work. But "evangelization" is the page's own vocabulary, not our five focus areas verbatim — hence `maybe`.

**What to do next.** Submit a Letter of Request through the Applicant Portal between Jan 1 and May 1 for next-year funding; coordinate the letter of support with archSA Stewardship (Grants Manager Cynthia Hoffman, 210-734-1606), allowing 2–3 weeks before the deadline per archSA guidance.
