**What it funds.** The Meadows Foundation (Dallas, founded 1948, one of Texas's largest private foundations) makes grants throughout Texas in six program areas — Arts & Culture, Civic & Public Affairs, Education, Environment, Health, and Human Services. Program/project grants are about 85% of giving; no deadlines exist, the online application is always open, and the board awards grants at five meetings a year (January, April, June, September, November) on a four-to-five-month cycle, with one proposal per organization per 12 months. Its Human Services area is the anchor for a diocesan family-life applicant: "In communities across Texas, people face overwhelming challenges and times of personal or family crisis. We support programs and agencies that thoughtfully weave compassion and respect into the support systems that improve the quality of life for the most vulnerable Texans."

**Why we qualify — and why this is a maybe.** Eligibility on the grantor's own page is stated generically — "qualified 501(c)(3) public charities or public entities serving the people of Texas" — statewide with no Bexar County exclusion. No religious-org exclusion appears, but no religious-org inclusion either (the FAQ speaks only of "qualified public entities and 501(c)(3) charities"), so per our qualification filter this is a maybe, not eligible. The match for our offices would be a family-strengthening, formation-adjacent program (marriage preparation reach, family outreach serving vulnerable Texans) framed with the measurable outcomes the page demands: quantitatively measurable outcomes, identified co-funders, and Meadows support "vital" to the project. Our grants-received records scan (2025–2027) shows no Meadows grants to ArchSA entities.

**What to do next.** Create an account in the Meadows application portal and submit one application (only one per 12-month period); apply at least four months before funds are needed. Before that, register for one of the monthly Human Services information sessions (45 minutes, day varies — registration form at mfi.org) and optionally call grants staff at 214-826-9431. Note the stated exclusions: no fundraising-event underwriting, annual drives, or conference costs outside initiative areas.
