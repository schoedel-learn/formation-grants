**What it funds.** Full-time service members (capacity builders, not volunteers) for
nonprofit projects serving low-income communities. AmeriCorps supplies the members
and living allowances; the sponsor designs the project, supervises members, and
funds small project costs. For the Archdiocese, the plausible shape is hosting
VISTAs to build self-sustaining capacity in family life, catechetical, or pastoral
ministry programs serving low-income parishes — e.g., building the administrative
skeleton for parish-level family formation outreach.

**Why we qualify — and why only maybe.** Sponsorship eligibility is explicit and a
diocesan office fits ("nonprofit organizations... eligible to apply to be
sponsors"). The ambiguity is the statutory purpose: verbatim from the sponsor
handbook, "AmeriCorps VISTA is an anti-poverty program that provides full-time and
summer associate service members to nonprofit organizations and local agencies that
serve low-income communities. The program's purpose, as defined by law, is to
strengthen efforts to eliminate poverty by encouraging people from all walks of
life to engage in meaningful volunteer service." A VISTA project must be anchored
in anti-poverty outcomes, so a formation-focused project only qualifies if it
demonstrably strengthens families in poverty rather than serving
formation/ministry programs generally. Also, no sponsor application window is open
today — AmeriCorps' forecast lists "VISTA: New Applications" targeting October 2026.

**What to do next.** Email AmeriCorpsGrants@AmeriCorps.gov (or the TTA hotline
800-942-2677) to reach the VISTA project officer for the region covering Texas; ask
when the next new-sponsor application window opens and whether a diocesan family
capacity project would pass the anti-poverty lens. Watch
americorps.gov/partner/funding-opportunities monthly for "VISTA: New Applications."
