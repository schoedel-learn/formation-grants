**What it funds.** Direct financial assistance to parishes and campus ministries for liturgical and musical purposes: hymnals and worship resources, instrument repair/replacement, bilingual worship materials and multicultural-ministry training, digital liturgy-preparation resources, and "attending or hosting musical or liturgical conferences and workshops." Running since 2001; more than $3.8 million donated to date. Individual award amounts are not published on the page, so budget expectations are small-scale (typically parish-level needs).

**Why we qualify.** Open to "all Roman Catholic parishes in the United States and U.S. territories" expressly, with no requirement to use OCP materials. The diocesan office cannot apply directly (grantee is the parish), but this is a zero-exclusion Catholic pool — ideal for routing to parishes with liturgy/catechesis formation needs, especially bicultural communities.

**What to do next.** The 2026 application closed; the page states "We will begin accepting applications in mid-January, 2027." Identify 3–5 archSA parishes in formation need, brief pastors before Christmas, and submit when the 2027 form opens. Grant letters go to the pastor's attention after the September decision.
