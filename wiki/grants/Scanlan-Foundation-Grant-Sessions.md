**What it funds.** Scanlan funds Roman Catholic organizations across the 15 Texas dioceses to build "a culture of vocations to the priesthood, religious life, and holy families," through two grant sessions a year (A and B). Its stated funding pillars include Marriage & Holy Families, Catholic Education, Evangelization & Discipleship, and Life & Human Dignity — marriage and family formation work is a named pillar, not an afterthought.

**Why we qualify.** Eligibility explicitly contemplates diocesan applicants: "(arch)dioceses may submit two unsolicited applications per calendar year," with a dedicated Diocese Grant Application Form. If our (arch)diocese is in Texas, this is a direct, unlimited-geography-within-Texas fit for family and marriage formation initiatives.

**What to do next.** The 2027A forms download from the grants page between November 1 and December 15, 2026; complete the Diocese Grant Application Form (plus W-9 and required attachments as individual PDFs) and email to grants@scanlanfoundation.org before December 15, 2026. Read the application guidelines first — decisions for 2027A come no later than June 1, 2027, and Session 2027B runs May 1–June 15, 2027.
