**What it funds.** Capacity-building grants (staff & program expansion, capital, equipment & technology) to 501(c)(3) nonprofits in five focus areas; the Civic Engagement & Community Services area includes a "Faith Formation" sector whose subsectors are Camps & Retreat Centers, Evangelism & Discipleship, International Missions, Marketplace Ministries, **Marriage & Family**, Seminaries & Bible Colleges, and Youth & Young Adult — a direct focus match for our family-life and marriage lanes. Faith communities (such as churches) are eligible entities "with a project that is primarily external facing, serving people beyond its own congregants." The Trust awarded $34,368,234 across 152 grants January–April 2026 (grants report), so awards are routinely six- and seven-figure scale.

**Why we don't qualify right now.** A hard geographic gate: a Strategic Project applicant must be "based in Alaska, Idaho, Montana, Oregon, or Washington — or your project has more than 50% impact in this region," and section C states "The Trust prioritizes grants to organizations based in Alaska, Idaho, Montana, Oregon, and Washington, or projects with greater than 50% impact to this region," with beyond-region support possible only "by Trust-initiated invite only." A San Antonio (arch)diocesan program cannot self-initiate; no Texas path exists in the current guidelines.

**What to do next.** No apply. Keep as watchlist intelligence: if ArchSA ever runs a Pacific Northwest program, or if Murdock's invite-only beyond-region practice surfaces a named diocesan cohort, revisit. Next LOI windows: Oct 15–Nov 11, 2026; Mar 1–Mar 29, 2027 (process page). Consider the Trust's Enrich loan option only in that same PNW-anchored frame.
