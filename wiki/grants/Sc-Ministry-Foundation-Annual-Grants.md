**What it funds.** The grantmaking foundation of the Sisters of Charity of Cincinnati (founded 1996; over $165 million awarded locally, regionally, nationally, and internationally). It funds nonprofits advancing the sisters' mission of service, justice, and compassion through initiatives rooted in Catholic Social Teaching — education, health care, social justice, environmental stewardship, and human dignity. From 2027 its annual grant cycle is administered by the Greater Cincinnati Foundation, with an LOI deadline of February 5, proposal deadline of March 26, and notice of decision June 1; grant terms must align with July 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028.

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan faith-formation or family-ministry initiative is a 501(c)(3) affiliate whose work is consistent with Catholic Social Teaching and the sisters' education and human-dignity commitments, but San Antonio is outside the Tri-State/Greater Cincinnati region where most grants go — outside-area funding depends on a Sisters of Charity connection or interest alignment, quoted verbatim, hence `maybe`.

**What to do next.** Plan to submit the LOI through the Greater Cincinnati Foundation online system before February 5 for the 2027 cycle (applications for the closed 2026 cycle were rejected as outside it); first contact GCF (513.241.2880) to confirm the outside-area pathway and whether an archdiocesan formation office fits the sisters' interest areas.
