**What it funds.** Program, project, and operating grants organized by monthly thematic areas; the Religion-themed month covers "Chaplains, Evangelism, [and] Seminaries," and the foundation's religion funding historically supports evangelization and mission-based work of the Los Angeles Roman Catholic community. The Foundation funds approximately 300 organizations per year, most with long standing relationships.

**Why we qualify.** An LA-area diocesan evangelization or catechesis effort fits the Religion thematic area and the foundation's Catholic mission, but the eligibility line is a hard geographic constraint — "Funding is generally limited to Los Angeles County" — hence `maybe` unless our (arch)diocese or a program of ours based outside LA operates in Los Angeles County.

**What to do next.** The next year's thematic application calendar is usually posted by end of October ("If the due date for your thematic area has passed, please wait to apply until the next year's application has been posted. This is usually done by the end of October."). Check the Grant Eligibility & Application page in early November 2026 for the new December-theme (Chaplains, Evangelism, Seminaries) deadline, then apply through the foundation's grant portal (grantinterface.com).
