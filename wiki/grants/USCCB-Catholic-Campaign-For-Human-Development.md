**What it funds.** CCHD gives annual grants to anti-poverty work — community development (organizing low-income people to change structures and policies) and economic development (businesses/jobs/assets owned by low-income communities). It is a program of the USCCB, funded by an annual parish collection, with applications run online after a Grant Eligibility and Inquiry Form stage, and every application requires coordination with the local CCHD diocesan director and bishop endorsement.

**Why we don't qualify.** The program's funded activities are anti-poverty organizing and economic development, not catechesis or faith formation; nothing in the CCHD purpose overlaps our focus areas (family, marriage, adult faith formation, OCIA). A formation apostolate that does not primarily organize low-income communities for economic change does not fit CCHD's mandate. If our apostolate ever launches a jobs/housing/food-access program led by low-income community members, revisit this page.

**What to do next.** None for now. If revisited, contact the local CCHD diocesan director first (listed on the USCCB CCHD Diocesan Coordinators page) and use the Guide for Grant Applicants as the entry document.
