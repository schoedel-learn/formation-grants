**What it funds.** Raskob's Domestic Grant Program makes grants to Catholic projects and programs that take place within the United States and its territories, through two open submission windows each year (January 1–15 and June 1–15, via its online grant portal). There is no stated restriction on ministry type beyond its listed exclusions (individuals, endowments, debt reduction, political activity), so family-life, marriage, adult catechesis, and OCIA/catechumenate programs are all fundable project categories.

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan office is a Catholic 501(c)(3) public charity listed in the Official Catholic Directory, and the required Letter from the Ordinary acknowledging the project is routine for a diocesan applicant — the bishop's letter is the core eligibility document and our office sits inside the Ordinary's structure.

**What to do next.** Register an account on Raskob's grant portal now (registration is open year-round even when the form is closed). Prepare the required documents: financial statement (Form 990), detailed project budget, IRS W-9, and the letter from the Ordinary. Submit during the January 1–15, 2027 window (5pm ET close); the form only appears once the window opens.
