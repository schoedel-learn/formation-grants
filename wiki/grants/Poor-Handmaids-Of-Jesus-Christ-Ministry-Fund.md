**What it funds.** Congregational funding from the Poor Handmaids of Jesus Christ (Donaldson, Indiana) for ministries and projects serving the economically poor and underserved, changing oppressive structures, and developing and enabling people — typically one-year program grants up to $15,000, evaluated quarterly. Equipment, construction, salaries, and administrative costs are excluded.

**Why we qualify.** The fund is open to outside organizations (not just PHJC-sponsored ministries) whose project furthers the sisters' mission, so a diocesan family-life program serving poor and marginalized families is a possible fit — but the mandatory endorsement from a PHJC Sister or Associate must be secured first, hence `maybe`.

**What to do next.** Identify a Poor Handmaid Sister or Associate who can attest to the proposed family-ministry work (check for PHJC presence or former ministries in Texas), then contact ministryfund@poorhandmaids.org before the next quarterly submission deadline.
