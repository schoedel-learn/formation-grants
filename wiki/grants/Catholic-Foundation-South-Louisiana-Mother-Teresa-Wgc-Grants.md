**What it funds.** The giving circle of the Houma-Thibodaux diocesan community foundation awards annual grants to nonprofits whose projects specifically impact women, children, and families — $153,000 to six nonprofits in 2024 and "$160,000+" in the May 2025 cycle (foundation news posts). The LOI opens December 1 and closes January 30, with an awards reception in May. Other foundation lines are diocesan-internal: ACTS retreat scholarships for Houma-Thibodaux Catholic school teachers (open year-round), tuition-assistance endowments for that diocese's Catholic elementary schools, and Fr. Willie Todd start-up grants for its parishes and schools.

**Why we qualify (partially).** The family focus matches our lane exactly, and applicants are "nonprofits" (not restricted to Catholic entities) — but the geographic test is "located within the Diocese of Houma-Thibodaux," so a San Antonio apostolate cannot qualify. Retain as a model: a women's giving circle at a small diocesan foundation funding family-impact work at meaningful size ($20k–$55k grants).

**What to do next.** Not actionable from San Antonio. If the archdiocese pursues a giving-circle model for family-life ministries in Texas, CFSL is the operating pattern to copy (LOI → committee review → membership vote).
