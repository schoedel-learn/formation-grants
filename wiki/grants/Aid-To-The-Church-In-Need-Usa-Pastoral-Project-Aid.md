**What it funds.** ACN funds pastoral initiatives, religious formation (seminaries, courses, scholarships), media apostolate, and construction/renovation through an open, rolling application process: a detailed project description, the local bishop's approval letter, cost estimate, financing plan, and forms submitted by email to a regional contact — with requests for pastoral programs and formation courses due at least three months before activities begin. No dollar range is published.

**Why we might qualify — and why this is only a maybe.** The eligibility text names diocesan and parish ecclesiastical authorities without a stated geographic restriction, and an archdiocesan formation office fits the applicant type (the bishop's approval letter is routine for us). But ACN's charter identity is aid to the suffering and persecuted Church; the page does not say whether needs inside a well-resourced US archdiocese are prioritized or funded. Status stays `maybe` pending a direct conversation with ACN's regional contact — do not prepare a full proposal before that call.

**What to do next.** Contact ACN USA (churchinneed.org) and ask one question: will ACN consider a US diocesan pastoral/formation project request? If yes, the international three-months-ahead rule makes ACN only viable for projects in 2027 planning windows.
