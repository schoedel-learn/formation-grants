**What it funds.** The Subcommittee on Catholic Home Missions funds operating expenses (not capital) for pastoral activities that strengthen or extend the Catholic Church's presence in the United States — its stated priority areas are "evangelization, catechesis, the promotion of parish life and the training of priests, deacons, religious and lay ministers." Grants may be centered at the parish, diocesan, interdiocesan or national level. There is no minimum request; the page states a maximum grant of $125,000. Actual 2025–2026 allocations to 69 mission dioceses ran roughly $10,000–$145,000 (per the USCCB announcement reported by OSV News, not the application page).

**Why we qualify (or don't).** This is US funding for US applicants and catechesis/faith-community formation is squarely within the eligible-activity list — but by the letter of the current guidelines the applicant must be the diocese itself, not a parish or apostolate: the Grant Guidelines page states verbatim, "AT THIS TIME, the Subcommittee is only approving grants to Class I applicants," where Class I is "Diocesan bishops of home mission dioceses." So a diocesan family/faith-formation office in a designated home mission diocese fits; a non-mission diocese only "in rare instances"; a parish or apostolate cannot apply directly.

**What to do next.** Confirm with the diocesan office whether our (arch)diocese is on the USCCB home mission list; if yes, the diocesan bishop's office applies — the online application site opens in early January each year and applications must be received by April 1 for October decisions (mid-November notification). Contact Ken Ong, USCCB Subcommittee on Catholic Home Missions, (202) 541-3400 or (202) 541-3010, before preparing anything.
