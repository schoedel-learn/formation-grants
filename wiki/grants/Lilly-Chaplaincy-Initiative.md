**What it funds.** Grants of up to $2.5 million to approximately 60 accredited four-year colleges and universities that already operate chaplaincies or offices of religious life, to strengthen how they nurture the religious lives of students. Two stages: concept papers due 2027-01-11 (information sessions Oct 29 and Nov 16), up to 120 schools invited to Stage 2 in March 2027 for invited implementation proposals.

**Why we don't qualify (for a diocesan applicant).** The applicant class is exclusively "accredited, four-year colleges and universities" — a diocese, parish, or diocesan ministry organization cannot apply. Noted here so the diocese knows the channel: the Campus Ministry Initiative (see companion page) is Lilly's parallel avenue for diocesan/organizational applicants, while this one belongs to any Catholic college in the diocese's orbit — worth forwarding the initiative page to a local Catholic institution if one wants the funding.

**What to do next.** No diocesan action; if a Catholic four-year college in the diocese is interested, send it the initiative page — its concept paper deadline is 2027-01-11.
