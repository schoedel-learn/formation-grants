**What it funds.** The San Antonio Area Foundation's Special and Urgent Needs (SUN) grant is its rapid-response channel: one-time grants of up to $10,000 for "an unexpected and unbudgeted need critical to operations," reviewed monthly with applications "submitted at any time." This is a different program from the regular Responsive/Community grants the Area Foundation has competitively awarded before. For 2026, SUN money was fully expended and applications are Closed "for the remainder of the 2026 calendar year"; the portal reopens January 1, 2027.

**Near-miss status — coordinate internally before applying.** Funder already supports other archSA entities — coordinate internally before applying: the Area Foundation's community-grants awards to Catholic Charities ($75,000, November 2025, recorded in wiki/Grants-Received-2025-2027.md) sit under the same institutional umbrella, which is why this page is a maybe rather than a new grantor. The SUN program itself is a distinct, smaller rapid-response fund we have demonstrably not received. Note the fit caveat honestly: SUN is general operational rapid response, not formation programming — reserve it for genuine unbudgeted emergencies (facility damage, lost funding mid-program) rather than planned formation work.

**What to do next.** Calendar the January 1, 2027 reopening. Because the Area Foundation already has archSA relationships, check with grants leadership before any submission so diocesan-level relationships stay coherent; contact Gavin Nichols (210) 242-4720 when the cycle reopens if a real urgent need exists.
