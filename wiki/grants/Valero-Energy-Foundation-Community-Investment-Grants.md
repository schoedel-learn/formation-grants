**What it funds.** The Valero Energy Foundation (Valero is headquartered in San Antonio) makes rolling
community-investment grants to 501(c)(3)s in Valero's major-operations communities through four pillars:
Basic Needs/Social Services, Health Care, Education, and Civic. Additionally, Valero's Benefit for
Children (BFC) program — funded by the Valero Texas Open, which raised $25.2 million in 2026 — grants
for "a specific program or project that aligns with one of Valero's three core charitable pillars"
(Basic Needs, Education, Healthcare) where "the request must directly benefit children from birth
through the age 18"; BFC grants do not fund general administrative, operating or maintenance expenses,
staff salaries, capital campaigns, or multi-year commitments.

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan 501(c)(3) office in San Antonio — Valero's headquarters city — fits the
generic 501(c)(3) test; a specific family-support project serving children (e.g., a parish-based
after-school feeding or family-support initiative) fits the Basic Needs/Social Services pillar.

**What to do next.** Submit an inquiry at https://valero.versaic.com/login, combining any multiple needs
in one application ("If you have more than one need for the year, please combine all of your needs in
one application") and timing the request at least two months before the program's own deadline; no
contact name is published, so establish the point of contact through the portal.
