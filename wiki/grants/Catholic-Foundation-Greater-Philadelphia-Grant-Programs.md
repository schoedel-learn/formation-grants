**What it funds.** CFGP runs a menu of small competitive programs for the 2026-2027 year: Vocations and Priestly Formation ($1,000–$2,500), Preservation and Liturgical Arts ($1,000–$2,500), Parish Life ($1,000–$2,500, 2 awards), Evangelization and Worship ($1,000–$2,500) — which "supports Catholic-aligned organizations and initiatives that foster a deeper encounter with Christ through prayer, worship, faith formation, and evangelization" — Education and Youth Development ($1,000–$2,500, 2 awards), Corporal Works of Mercy ($1,000–$2,500, 2 awards), Women in Philanthropy (1 award, deadline January 22, 2027, amount not stated), and the invitation-only Alice Mayer endowment ($15,000–$25,000). Most deadlines were October 9, 2026.

**Why we qualify (partially).** Eligibility is Catholic alignment + 501(c)(3) + institutional approval — but the proposed work must "benefit the Delaware Valley community," which excludes San Antonio-based work. The Evangelization and Worship program's explicit faith-formation language makes this the best focus match in the Mid-Atlantic; geography is the only barrier.

**What to do next.** Only actionable if a Delaware Valley entity applies. The WiP deadline (January 22, 2027) is the next open competitive window on the page.
