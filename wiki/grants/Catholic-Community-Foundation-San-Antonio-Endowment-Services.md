**What it offers.** This is San Antonio's own Catholic community foundation (founded 2006 by episcopal decree, independently incorporated, $165M+ in assets under management, 170+ funds; Archbishop Gustavo Garcia-Siller sits ex-officio). Its services are fund stewardship: endowments, donor-advised funds, and faith-aligned investment accounts — income distributions flow to the designated entity each year. Its only structured new-money program of record is a Parish Endowments Matching Program: a first parish endowment matched 1:1 up to $5,000, as listed on the archdiocese's own Grants & Giving resources.

**Why we do not qualify.** There is no competitive formation-grant cycle: the foundation is an endowment/DAF administrator, not a grantmaker with an application process for ministry programs. Per the lane rules it is recorded here with status `not_eligible` so the absence is documented. Note the constructive path it does offer: establishing a designated endowment (funds from $10,000) would create annual income for any archSA family/marriage/formation ministry — a durable funding instrument rather than a grant.

**What to do next.** Contact ccfinfo@ccftx.org / (210) 732-2157 to discuss a designated endowment for the archdiocese's formation ministries, and confirm current matching-program terms (the archSA deck lists it; the foundation's site no longer details it).
