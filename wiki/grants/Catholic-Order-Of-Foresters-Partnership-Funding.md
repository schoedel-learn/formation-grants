**What it funds.** Each COF Local Court selects one major cause per year, raises funds, and the High Court matches dollar-for-dollar up to $2,500 ("$1,000 is the minimum partnered amount available from the High Court"). Program goals target "significant diocesan, seminary, and Catholic educational appeals" — exactly the profile of a diocesan faith-formation, seminary, or Catholic-education appeal. The fraternal's Matching Funds events mirror this: "Local courts may hold only one Matching Funds event per year, and each event must be approved."

**Why we qualify — with a caveat.** The recipient (a diocesan office, seminary appeal, or Catholic education program) never applies: the Local Court completes the Partnership Funding Application (form FA-225-A), mails it to the Home Office for High Court approval at least 30 days out, then fundraises. This is pure member-sponsorship funding — the diocese's job is to have a healthy relationship with nearby COF courts, not to file a proposal. Member sponsorship required → maybe under lane rules; the payoff is small (max $2,500) but the paper trail is one form.

**What to do next.** Identify COF local courts in the diocese (the society is licensed in 34 states plus D.C.), present the appeal to a court's officers, and ask them to file FA-225-A naming the diocesan program as recipient. Direct questions to Judy James (jjames@catholicforester.org), the named Matching Funds contact on the Fraternal Outreach page.
