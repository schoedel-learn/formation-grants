**What it funds.** The Society for the Propagation of the Faith is one of the four Pontifical Mission Societies (with the Missionary Childhood Association, Society of St. Peter Apostle, and Missionary Union). Its US national office raises funds through World Mission Sunday and other appeals and channels them as subsidies to mission dioceses and missionaries in the "Pope's missions" — young churches in mission territories abroad (food, education, medical care, parish support, seminarians, catechists).

**Why we don't qualify.** This is not a US domestic grantor for formation programs: its funds are directed to mission territories outside the United States, and it is a pontifical society (US national office in New York) rather than a USCCB-administered program — the lane checked it because it is sometimes mistaken for one. A parish-based or diocesan formation apostolate in the US is outside both the funding geography and the recipient structure.

**What to do next.** None for US formation funding. If the interest is mission cooperation instead, the practical US pathway is each diocese's Missionary Cooperative Plan (Missionary Cooperation Appeal), which allocates parish speaking slots — handled through the diocesan mission office, not a national application.
