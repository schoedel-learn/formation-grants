**What it funds.** Federal grants for "healthy marriage promotion" — curriculum-based
marriage and relationship education/skills, pre-marital education, marriage
enhancement, divorce reduction, marriage mentoring, public campaigns, and activities
to reduce disincentives to marriage — for adult individuals and adult couples (age
18 and older), combined with help removing barriers to participation and family
economic stability services. ACF/OFA funds four HMRF programs at $150 million per
year; the September 2025 cohort made 109 five-year awards across 38 states. The
HEART opportunity was part of that cohort: $34,650,000 program funding, 40 expected
awards, $400,000–$1,250,000 per applicant, 5-year project period, cooperative
agreement, no match.

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan family life/marriage office is a nonprofit applicant
with 501(c)(3) status, and the listing states faith-based organizations are eligible
and may apply on the same basis as any other organization.

**What to do next.** The FY 2025 cohort (awarded 2025-09-29, running to 2030) means
the next open competition is likely a few years out: subscribe to Grants.gov updates
for Assistance Listing 93.086 and set a calendar check each quarter; meanwhile the
Archdiocese's family life office can benchmark against current Texas HMRF grantees
on the ACF project map. Contact Taffy Compain, ofa@grantreview.org, to ask when the
next HEART-scope NOFO is forecast.
