**What it funds.** The Moody Foundation (Galveston, founded 1942, one of Texas's largest private foundations) runs a standing General Grant Inquiry process through its Grants Portal: an inquiry form with an eligibility quiz, then — for projects that fit its Texas priorities of "education, social services and family well-being, health and wellness, and the arts" — a full application requested by staff. Inquiries are welcome year-round; trustees review at four regular meetings a year, and inquiry-to-decision commonly takes up to six months. Only one active grant per organization is allowed.

**Why we qualify — and why this is a maybe.** "Social services and family well-being" is a named Texas priority, which maps directly onto our family-life lane, and the process is open, rolling, and statewide (grants need only be expended in Texas — no Galveston-area residency test is stated). What is not stated anywhere on the grantor's own pages is whether explicitly religious/Catholic programming is fundable — no faith language appears — so per our qualification filter this is a maybe, not eligible. The archdiocesan family-life office could frame a family-strengthening program (marriage preparation reach, family outreach), presenting formation outcomes without hiding its diocesan identity. Our grants-received records scan (2025–2027) shows no Moody grants to ArchSA entities.

**What to do next.** Create an account in the Moody Foundation Grants Portal and submit one inquiry (portal enforces a single active inquiry). Budget staff lead time: if an inquiry is accepted, a full application follows, then compliance review before a trustee meeting — up to six months end to end. Amounts are not published on the application-process page.
