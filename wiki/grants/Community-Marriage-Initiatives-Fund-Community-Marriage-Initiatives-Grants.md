**What it funds.** Capacity-building grants to help non-profit organizations replicate a specific
community-wide marriage ministry model ("Vision, Skills and Support" across the marital life-cycle,
funding at roughly $1 per resident per year) so local churches implement self-sustaining marriage
ministries and county divorce rates fall. Awards run up to $30,000 in Year 1, $40,000 in Year 2 and
$30,000 in Year 3 — "a maximum grant allocation from the CMI Fund up to $100,000 to fund a project
that will span three years." Rounds have recurred since 2022 (seven rounds before the eighth).

**Why we qualify — with a caveat.** The page names no diocesan applicant category: eligibility is
simply non-profit organizations in the US, and the funded work is expanding church-based marriage
ministry at community scale, which a diocesan Marriage & Family Life office naturally fits. But the
intent targets "non-profit organizations who already have some track record of assisting churches
implement marriage ministries" — a community-organization profile, not explicitly a diocesan one,
so status is kept at maybe until the grantor confirms a diocesan office is acceptable.

**What to do next.** Round 8 closed September 15, 2026 (results announced October 15, 2026; grant
period began October 16, 2026) and no Round 9 had been posted as of the last check. Contact the
administrator at the page's published address to ask when the next round opens and whether a
diocesan office applicant is welcome before drafting anything.
