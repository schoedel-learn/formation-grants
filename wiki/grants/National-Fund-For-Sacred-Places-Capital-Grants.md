**What it funds.** Matching capital grants ($50,000–$500,000) for significant capital projects — restoration, rehabilitation, major repair — at historic houses of worship, open to congregations of any faith tradition, with annual application, training, and a two-stage selection into the grantee cohort (typically 30–40 per year). Note on exclusions: the program is funded by Lilly Endowment, but this page tracks the National Fund as its own funder, distinct from the Lilly-Endowment-as-grantor lane.

**Not eligible — one-line reason.** Matching capital grants for historic-building preservation projects sit outside all five focus areas (family, marriage, adult faith formation, faith formation, OCIA) — the funded work is architecture and restoration, not formation programming.

**Boss note.** If a historic diocesan church ever needs restoration capital, the cycle is real and dated: applications open in Foundant on January 12, 2027 and are due March 3, 2027 at 11:59 pm EST, decisions October 2027. Kept in the corpus for that future scenario, not for the current formation-formation push.
