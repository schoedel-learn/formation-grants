**What it funds.** NCPD does not run a competitive grant program. Its funded offering to dioceses and parishes is the Lilly-backed Making Parishes Everyone's Home formation program: two-year mentorship cohorts pairing parish/diocesan leadership teams with experienced disability-ministry dioceses, all-participant funding to attend the national conference, training, and resource development. The standing open door is affiliate membership ($1,500/year per the 2026–2027 form) with a Mission Diocese Scholarship Application (due June 12, 2026 for the 2026–2027 cycle), which discounts membership for mission dioceses.

**Why we qualify.** Participation is built for exactly a diocesan-office/parish-team application, and the formation content (sacramental access, adaptive catechesis training, parish advocacy programs) sits inside faith formation for Catholics with disabilities — but there is no cash grant to capture, so this page is a program-tracking placeholder rather than a funding line.

**What to do next.** The three-year Lilly plan (2024–2026) is in its final stretch and the first mentorship cohort is fully seated; watch ncpd.org and the NCPD newsletter for a successor cohort or new intake, and in the meantime have the diocesan disability contact join the monthly NCPD Affiliate network if our diocese is not already on the affiliates list. Email ncpd@ncpd.org to ask about the next cohort intake.
