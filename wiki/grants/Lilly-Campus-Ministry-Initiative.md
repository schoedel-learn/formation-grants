**What it funds.** New 2026 Lilly initiative granting up to $150 million total to roughly 20 national/regional organizations that support local Christian campus ministries across multiple campuses — Newman Center-style Catholic campus ministry is squarely in scope ("denominationally based and supported student ministries" and "congregation-based student ministries hosted by churches or parishes near campuses" are named forms). Stage 1 concept papers due 2027-01-25, with $50,000 planning grants (~20) in March 2027; Stage 2 implementation grants of up to $7.5 million each over up to five years — up to $5 million program costs plus up to $2.5 million on a 1:1 matching basis to build a fund for local campus ministries. Implementation proposals invited; due 2027-06-07; awards announced October 2027.

**Why we qualify.** The applicant must be an organization supporting campus ministries at MULTIPLE campuses — exactly the profile of a diocesan Office of Campus Ministry / young-adult ministry serving several university Newman centers or parish-based campus ministries in the diocese; the RFP's tax-status section explicitly contemplates "regional judicatories and church networks" (the Catholic analog being a diocese). A single-campus parish Newman center or one campus ministry alone is NOT eligible — the diocesan office must be the applicant.

**What to do next.** Inventory the diocese's campus ministries (how many campuses, which form) to confirm the multi-campus story, register for the Nov 2 or Nov 16 information session, and prepare the concept-paper package (one-page request letter, information form, ≤1-page executive summary, ≤7-page narrative, $50,000 planning budget) for submission by 2027-01-25 via the form on the initiative page. Questions: campusministry@lei.org.
