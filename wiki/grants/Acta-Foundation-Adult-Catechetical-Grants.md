**What it funds.** Creation, development, and production of catechetical projects that foster adult faith development in the Roman Catholic tradition — "innovative catechetical initiatives for adults that will have a broad impact on the life of the Roman Catholic Church," ordinarily in the $5,000–$50,000 range. Operational costs and salaries are excluded; this is project money, e.g. developing an OCIA/adult-catechesis resource or program, not staffing.

**Why we qualify.** The archdiocesan office is a U.S. Catholic organization and adult catechesis (adult faith formation, OCIA) is exactly the funder's stated project type. The fall deadline — November 13, 2026 — is under four weeks out, making this the most time-sensitive page this lane produced.

**What to do next.** Pick one concrete adult-catechesis product/program idea (never an operating budget), itemize the project budget, gather the office's overall budget and any sample material, and submit through the online application before November 13, 2026. One grant per organization at a time, and the funded material copy must be delivered to ACTA on completion. Contact is via the web form on actafoundation.org/contact-us.
