**What it funds.** Federal grants for "healthy marriage promotion" aimed at youth and
young adults: skills-based healthy relationship and marriage education (HMRE),
education in high schools, and public advertising campaigns, serving youth in high
school (grades 9–12), high-school-aged youth (ages 14–17), and/or youth in late
adolescence to early adulthood (ages 18–24) — including pregnant or parenting youth,
youth in foster care or aging out of it, and youth in at-risk environments — plus
parenting and job and career advancement supports for young families. The
READY4Life opportunity was part of the September 2025 cohort: $22,000,000 program
funding, 26 expected awards, $400,000–$1,250,000 per applicant, 5-year project
period, cooperative agreement, no match.

**Why we qualify.** The NOFO's own eligibility list includes nonprofits with or
without 501(c)(3) status, and states that faith-based organizations meeting the
standard eligibility requirements are eligible — a diocesan youth/family formation
office qualifies.

**What to do next.** The FY 2025 cohort (awarded 2025-09-29, running to 2030) means
the next open competition is likely a few years out: subscribe to Grants.gov updates
for Assistance Listing 93.086 and check quarterly. Contact Toya Joyner
(OFA@grantreview.org) about the next forecast. If the Archdiocese wants youth
relationship education sooner, current Texas HMRF grantees on the ACF project map
can model what an evidence-based curricula partnership looks like.
