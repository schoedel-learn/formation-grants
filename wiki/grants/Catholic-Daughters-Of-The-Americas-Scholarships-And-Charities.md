**What it funds.** Nothing for outside applicants — verified absence, as requested. CDA is a grant-giving, not grant-seeking, organization: local courts and members donate to the order's preselected National Projects and Charities (via a member donation form), and CDA's own financial awards run entirely inward — national scholarships and education contests for its member daughters/juniors, with forms posted in the members-only quarterly.

**Verified absent: the court/affiliate application path.** There is no court-, circle-, or affiliate-application mechanism by which a diocesan office, parish, or apostolate applies to receive CDA funding. A court can voluntarily designate a donation to an outside charity (that is a court decision, not an external application), but no published application, deadline, or grant program exists for Catholic organizations. Do not spend effort drafting proposals here.

**Why a page exists.** The lane brief explicitly asked whether ANY court/affiliate application path exists. It does not, per the grantor's own site, so the absence is documented so future research passes do not re-trace the question.
