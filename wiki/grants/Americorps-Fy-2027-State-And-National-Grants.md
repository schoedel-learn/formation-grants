**What it funds.** Grants that engage AmeriCorps members in evidence-based or
evidence-informed community service. The FY 2027 NOFO prioritizes consideration
(from the official funding-opportunity page) for: "Organizations that are
faith-based" and "Focus on strengthening families, e.g., activities that aim to
support low-income parents through parenting education, responsible parenting and
healthy relationship skills," plus communities with concentrated poverty, rural
communities, youth mental health, veterans, public safety, and workforce pathways.
A diocesan-sponsored corps could place members directly in family life, marriage
preparation, or religious education programs serving low-income parishes. Members
receive living allowances and education awards; the grant funds member-related costs,
with a cost-sharing/matching requirement (match waivers available). New applicants
are eligible for cost reimbursement, Education Award Program (EAP), and Professional
Corps grants but not full cost fixed amount grants.

**Why we qualify.** "Nonprofit organizations" plainly covers a diocesan office with
501(c)(3) status, faith-based organizations are the first named funding priority,
and the strengthening-families priority matches the family/marriage focus directly.

**What to do next.** Contact the Texas State Service Commission (OneStar — see the
AmeriCorps commissions webpage) immediately: single-state commission deadlines and
requirements precede the January 13, 2027 national deadline. Decide the first
project (e.g., AmeriCorps members expanding parish catechetical/family ministry in
under-resourced parishes) and whether cost reimbursement or EAP fits; use the
AmeriCorps Evidence Exchange to align the intervention. Hotline 800-942-2677.
