**What it funds.** Annual general-operating grants in the $3,000–$10,000 range for organizations serving the most vulnerable in the St. Louis region, with "Catholic and/or faith-oriented" organizations an explicit priority. The two-stage cycle runs from an April orientation webinar to an LOI in late May and a full application in early September, with awards announced the week of October 19.

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan family-life or outreach ministry serving vulnerable families — mothers, youth, immigrants — aligns with the "Catholic and/or faith-oriented" priority and the youth/women/immigrant funding priorities, subject to the hard geographic requirement, quoted verbatim below, hence `maybe`.

**What to do next.** The 2026 cycle is closed (awards announced week of October 19, 2026; spending in 2027). Mark late April 2027 to catch the next orientation webinar and LOI window; check iwfdn.org/grants in the spring for the 2027 timeline, and confirm with info@iwfdn.org whether an archdiocesan office qualifies or a parish/ministry partner should apply with fiscal sponsorship.
