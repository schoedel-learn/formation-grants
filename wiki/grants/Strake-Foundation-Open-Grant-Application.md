**What it funds.** The Strake Foundation of Houston ($110M+ granted since its 1952 founding, ~400 requests per year) is explicitly Catholic-guided: its mission is to "support conservative Catholic causes," and the first of six Donor Intent priorities is "Judeo-Christian Religious Organizations — With priority given to Catholic institutions." The Foundation's stated principles include "faithful formation," and the remaining priorities (hospitals/pro-life medical, private secondary and higher education, military/liberty causes, poverty initiatives, civic/cultural) all take secondary consideration. Applications are accepted year-round by email, and the Board meets three times a year (February, July, November) to make determinations.

**Why we qualify.** ArchSA formation offices and ministries are Catholic institutions — Strake's top-priority class — and faith/adult formation programming sits squarely in the Foundation's donor intent. Unlike most Texas Catholic funders, Strake states no geographic restriction on the religious-organizations priority: the geographic cues on the page ("priority given to Harris County," Houston first) attach to the poverty and civic/cultural items, not to item 1. Our grants-received records scan (2025–2027) shows no Strake grant to Archdiocese of San Antonio entities, so this is a genuinely new relationship.

**What to do next.** Download the application PDF from the grant-application page, submit as a single PDF to Foundation@strake.org, and confirm receipt within 1 business day. With the November 2026 board meeting as the next decision point, submit promptly; timing after that rolls to the February 2027 meeting. Amounts are not published on the site — anchor requests modestly and verify with staff if offered the chance.
