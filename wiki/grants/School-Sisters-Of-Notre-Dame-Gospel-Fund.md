**What it funds.** One grant round per fiscal year from the SSND Atlantic-Midwest Province for U.S. projects serving youth, women, and the poor in the congregation's spirit of justice — explicitly excluding the province's own sponsored/co-sponsored ministries, so the fund exists to support outside organizations. Maximum $7,500 per project; recipients report one year after funding.
