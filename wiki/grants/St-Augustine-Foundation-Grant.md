**What it funds.** The Order of St. Augustine's foundation "expresses the desire of Augustinians to be of service to God's people, to share resources and to participate in the Church's mission of evangelization," making annual one-time or multi-year (up to three years) grants to U.S. 501(c)(3)s in four areas of interest: community building and pastoral care, care of newcomers, education, and response to the needy. It prefers projects that become self-sustaining and does not fund ordinary operating budgets.

**Why we qualify.** The foundation funds the Church's evangelization mission beyond Augustinian institutions, and the "community building and pastoral care" and "Education" areas can carry parish/diocesan faith formation and family ministry — but every request must clear an Augustinian endorsement gate (endorser must be a member of the Order or Augustinian-entity leadership), hence `maybe`.

**What to do next.** Email David L. Canedo (dcanedo@thestaugustinefoundation.com, 619-564-8715) to screen a proposed catechesis/formation request for fit and identify an Augustinian endorser; applications available January 1 and due June 30, with notification by October 15 and checks mailed by November 15 (the page lists these dates without a year).
