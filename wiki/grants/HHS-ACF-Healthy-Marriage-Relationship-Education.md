**What it funds.** ACF's "HEART" cooperative agreements fund projects that "include a broad array of service provision strategies ... designed to support family strengthening activities through one or more of seven activities specified under the authorizing legislation: marriage and relationship education/skills (MRES); pre-marital education; marriage enhancement; divorce reduction activities; marriage mentoring; public advertising campaigns; and activities to reduce the disincentives to marriage" (FY2025 HEART listing). Projects must deliver at least 12 hours of evidence-informed, skills-based curriculum workshops in multiple sessions over at least two weeks, for adults 18+, with job/career-advancement add-ons (ACF program page). FY2025 HEART round: $34,650,000, 40 expected awards, award floor $400,000 and ceiling $1,250,000, no cost share; cooperative agreement (5-year federal project periods typical of HMRF).

**Why we qualify — and why this is a maybe.** Faith-based organizations are named explicitly and on equal footing: "Faith-based organizations may apply for this award on the same basis as any other organization ... ACF will not, in the selection of recipients, discriminate against an organization on the basis of the organization's religious character, affiliation, or exercise," and the seven allowed activities are the family-life/marriage office's core work (pre-marital education, marriage enhancement, marriage mentoring). A diocesan office would pair its marriage-prep and mentor programs with an approved evidence-informed curriculum and secular service outcomes (financial-management, career modules). The hold: the FY2025 HEART FOA closed July 29, 2025 (listing marked Closed, last updated May 13, 2026) and no next HMRE/HMRF competition is open as of 2026-10-27 — this is a watch-and-prepare recurring federal line, not a live window.

**What to do next.** Watch grants.gov (CFDA 93.086 Healthy Marriage Promotion and Responsible Fatherhood) and https://www.acf.hhs.gov/ofa/grants-funding/funding-opportunities for the next competitive HMRE/HMRF FOA cycle (NOFOs have recurred 2022, 2024, 2025); in the meantime, select a listed evidence-informed MRES curriculum and compile evaluation capacity so the office can move inside a ~2-week NOFO response window when the next competition posts.
