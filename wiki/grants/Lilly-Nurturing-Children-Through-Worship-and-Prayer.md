**What it funds.** Grants that help Christian congregations design intergenerational worship and prayer that intentionally engages children — inclusive of children with disabilities, arts/music, Bible storytelling, and connecting worship to children's daily family lives. The 2024 open competitive round (proposals up to $1.25 million over five years) closed in 2024 and produced 91 grants including Catholic ones. A successor, "Nurturing Children Through Worship and Prayer Initiative II," is actively awarding grants of roughly $1.25 million directly to Catholic dioceses — the Endowment's own Recent Grants page lists the Archdiocese of Indianapolis, the Diocese of El Paso Pastoral Center ($1,250,000, "Nurturing God's Children Through Worship and Prayer"), the Diocese of Fort Wayne ($1,250,000, "Alive in God's Word"), the Diocese of Lafayette-in-Indiana, and Catholic Extension ($1,250,000, "La Familia Catechesis") as II grantees.

**Why this is a "maybe" for us.** A diocesan office is a qualifying applicant type for this program (organizations supporting congregations; dioceses have won under II), but there is no public call visible: the main initiative page states "This initiative is currently closed to new proposals," no Initiative II RFP exists on lillyendowment.org, and no submission deadline is published — II appears to operate on invitations. We qualify on substance; the open question is whether II (or a III) will solicit an expression of interest from new applicants.

**What to do next.** Email nurturingchildren@lei.org asking whether Initiative II is still adding new grantees and how a diocese expresses interest; monitor the initiative page and the Recent Grants list monthly (a third competitive round is plausible given the 2024 pattern). If asked, have a short concept ready: one intergenerational liturgy/catechesis program serving multiple parishes in the diocese.
