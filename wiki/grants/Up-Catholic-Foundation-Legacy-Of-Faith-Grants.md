**What it funds.** The Legacy of Faith endowment — the largest fund of the U.P. Catholic Foundation (41 funds, $41.6M) — invites applications each Jan 1–April 1 window from parishes, missions, and ministries of the Diocese of Marquette for "evangelization and faith formation programs and materials," framing grantees around the bishop's three-step vision of forming Spirit-filled evangelizers (be a friend of Jesus, make a friend, introduce your friend to Jesus). Grant reports are due June 30 of the funding year. A separate St. Elizabeth fertility-assistance grant helps married couples facing fertility obstacles, in line with Church teaching.

**Why we qualify (partially).** The focus match is exact — faith formation and evangelization materials and programs — but eligibility is locked to the Diocese of Marquette's parishes, missions, and ministries, excluding a San Antonio applicant. Retain as a model of a small diocese running a calendarized faith-formation endowment cycle (Jan 1–Apr 1 apply; July 1–Jun 30 program year; June 30 report).

**What to do next.** Not actionable from San Antonio. The Jan 1–April 1 apply / July–June spend / June 30 report rhythm is a workable template if archSA seeks to stand up a domestic formation endowment.
