**What it funds.** Federal demonstration grants for "activities to promote
responsible fatherhood" across the three statutory activity areas — promoting or
sustaining marriage, responsible parenting, and economic stability — for adult
fathers/father figures age 18 and older with children age 24 or younger, including
community fathers and fathers returning from incarceration. Projects integrate
economic stability services with healthy marriage education and responsible
parenting supports. The FORGE opportunity was part of the September 2025 cohort:
$55,000,000 program funding, 66 expected awards, $400,000–$1,250,000 per applicant,
5-year project period, cooperative agreement, no match.

**Why we qualify.** The listing names "public and nonprofit community entities
(including religious organizations)" as eligible; a diocesan family life or prison
reentry ministry office is such an entity with 501(c)(3) status.

**What to do next.** The FY 2025 cohort (awarded 2025-09-29, running to 2030) means
the next open competition is likely a few years out: subscribe to Grants.gov
updates for Assistance Listing 93.086 and check quarterly; if fatherhood ministry in
the Archdiocese wants to engage sooner, Tanya Howell (ofa@grantreview.org) can point
to the next forecast and to current Texas FORGE grantees on the ACF project map.
