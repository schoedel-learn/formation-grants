**What it funds.** Dallas's Catholic community foundation — one of the largest in Texas — awards ~$2.4M each spring and fall from its Philanthropy Fund and endowments to Dallas-area Catholic schools, churches, and nonprofits; requests up to $100,000, with a Letter of Intent gateway. Current cycle: LOIs opened September 1 and are due Friday, November 13, 2026 for the Spring 2027 grant cycle (contact Ann Letteer). Site visits are conducted by trustees for all grant requests, and grantees must sit out two years before re-applying.

**Why we do not qualify.** Despite being a Texas funder, eligibility is fixed to organizations in the Diocese of Dallas — or organizations "serving an identifiable Catholic population" in Dallas with written support from Dallas-diocese clergy. A San Antonio-based apostolate serves no Dallas Catholic population and therefore cannot meet the stated test; `not_eligible` even under the Texas-first lens. Retained because it is the dominant Catholic community-foundation grant cycle in Texas and a useful benchmark for LOI design (LOI → invited application → trustee site visit → grants announced within two weeks of the fall/spring board decision).

**What to do next.** No action for archSA. If any archSA-affiliated entity ever serves the Dallas area, the LOI deadline is November 13 annually for spring awards.
