**What it funds.** A 1:1 matching grant of up to $1,000 per event, applied for by any chapter, lodge, council, or volunteer team across the Trusted Fraternal Life brands (the family that absorbed Catholic Financial Life in 2025 and includes Catholic United Financial). The match flows to "a new 501(c)(3) cause aligned with Trusted Fraternal Life values" that the chapter fundraises for — the beneficiary organization itself is not restricted: page examples name food shelves, shelters, pro-life organizations, and "a new group within a parish."

**Why we qualify — with a caveat.** Two gates. First, the applicant is the member group, not the diocese: the beneficiary 501(c)(3) (e.g., a diocesan family-life or faith-formation office) is matched, not applied-for — member sponsorship, so maybe under lane rules. Second, the page names no focus areas at all — a beneficiary cause merely needs to "align with Trusted Fraternal Life values" — so the family/faith-formation focus is our mapping of the beneficiary's project, not the grantor's wording. The window is 2026-only ("one-time matching grant opportunity available in 2026"), and the current date sits inside it.

**What to do next.** Before end of 2026, a diocesan office can connect with a local TFL-brand council or volunteer team to design a new fundraiser whose beneficiary is the office's 501(c)(3) programming; the chapter files the Pre-Event Application from this page, then a Post-Event Summary releases the match. Contact Member Engagement via the brand's volunteer landing pages since the page's own applicant links route through brand sites.
