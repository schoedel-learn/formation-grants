**What it funds.** Southeast Michigan's Catholic community foundation runs one of the most open processes in this lane: any Michigan 501(c)(3) whose mission aligns with Catholic teaching may submit an online LOI (June 1–30), with invited applications due August 31 and awards made at the December board meeting. The Education/Formation line funds "innovative formation and education programs that reach people of all ages and enhance vibrant discipleship." Other named lines (Church in the City, Spirit of Innovation) are Archdiocese of Detroit-restricted.

**Why we qualify (partially).** The core Impact Grant eligibility does not require a diocesan affiliation — but it does require serving Michigan, which a San Antonio-based apostolate does not. If the archdiocese ever pilots work in Michigan, or partners with a Michigan-based Catholic apostolate, this is a template-simple process; otherwise it is out of range. Not a Texas funder.

**What to do next.** Only pursue if a Michigan-serving entity applies. Otherwise keep as a benchmark for how an open Catholic community foundation structures a two-stage LOI cycle.
