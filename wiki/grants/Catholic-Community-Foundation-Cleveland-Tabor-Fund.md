**What it funds.** The Catholic Community Foundation (Diocese of Cleveland) manages a small set of annual funds for diocesan agencies, schools, parishes, and programs within the eight-county diocese. The Tabor Fund is the relevant line: modest awards (up to $1,500) for personal/ministry formation — education and formation programs, sabbaticals, and retreats for ministry staff — paid to the sponsoring organization.

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan-office formation program is an "attendant organization engaged in a recognized ministry," and the award purpose (formation and renewal of ministry personnel) is squarely in our lane — but only if our diocese is (or has ministries in) the Diocese of Cleveland, hence `maybe`.
