**What it funds.** The Roman Catholic Foundation is the Archdiocese of St. Louis's endowment-planning foundation. Its active programs are Beyond Sunday tuition scholarships (elementary and high school) funded by the Beyond Sunday Education Fund, plus planned-giving and fund establishment services. Its general grant program exists only as archived past-recipient lists through 2020; today the only lay pathway to RCF grant dollars is the St. Martha Giving Circle, where members vote on beneficiaries each spring and organizations are nominated into the process rather than applying.

**Why we do not qualify.** No open parish/ministry application cycle exists at the check date — this page is written so a director does not assume a St. Louis "Catholic Foundation" means open formation funding. Family tuition assistance flows to families/students via schools, not to diocesan formation offices.

**What to do next.** If our diocese is Archdiocese of St. Louis–adjacent work with parishes: know that a St. Martha Giving Circle nomination (via rcfstl.org staff, themarthas@rcfstl.org) is the only plausible route, decided by member vote each spring — low predictability, not a calendar-driven application. Otherwise skip.
