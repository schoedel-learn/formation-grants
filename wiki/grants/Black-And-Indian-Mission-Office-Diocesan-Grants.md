**What it funds.** Annual grants to dioceses, schools, and religious-education programs supporting evangelization of African American, Native American, and Alaska Native Catholic communities. The Diocesan Grant list of fundable items verbatim includes "Faith formation programs for African American and Native American children and adults" alongside evangelization/ministry programs, tuition assistance, teacher and catechist stipends, catechetical materials and Bibles, and rural parish subsidies. The Office also runs two parallel application tracks — BCIM (Native American/Alaska Native communities) and CNAMB (Catholic African American communities) — open to Schools and Religious Education Programs for teacher salaries and catechist stipends. This is the USCCB-adjacent national Black and Indian Mission Collection regranted competitively: a collection round NOT covered by the already-tracked USCCB lanes.

**Why we qualify.** The applicant named is the diocese itself — "open to all Dioceses with African American and Native American/Alaska Native communities" — the cleanest diocesan-office eligibility in the corpus. Faith formation for children and adults is named verbatim as a fundable use.

**What to do next.** The page states the grant applications "for the calendar year January 1 to December 31, 2027 are now closed" — the 2027 cycle has shut as of this check, so contact Elizabeth Njai (202-331-8542 / enjai@blackandindianmission.org) for the 2028 cycle opening and required bishop-approval format. Amounts are not published on the page.
