**What it funds.** The Women's Giving Circle of the Archdiocese of New Orleans combines members' gifts into pooled grants — over $200,000 expected in 2027 — for nonprofits serving the eight civil parishes of the archdiocese, with grants covering program operational costs including materials and mission-related staff salaries (no capital, events, or deficits). The 2027 impact area is literally "Youth, Young Adults, and the Family (Faith Formation and Human Formation)."

**Why we qualify.** The 2027 impact area is a direct hit for a diocesan family-life and faith-formation office, and past WGC cycles funded archdiocesan offices (the Office of Marriage & Family Life and the Office of Black Catholic Ministries won in 2024). The catch: the 2027 eligibility wording excludes "schools and parishes" (quoted below), and says nonprofits — so `maybe` until an archdiocesan office's directory listing and eligibility are confirmed.

**What to do next.** The application window is open now (October 1–November 30, 2026). Call Kathi Zimmerman (504.527.5794, kzimmerman@ccfnola.org) immediately to confirm that our archdiocesan office is an eligible applicant under the Clarion Herald directory rule, then choose one of the three award-tier categories and apply before November 30.
