Proposed grants of up to $2.5 million over up to five years; ~60 awards anticipated and to be announced December 2026.

**Why we don't qualify right now.** The submission window closed May 18, 2026. Catholic dioceses were within the eligible applicant class (501(c)(3) public charities with a demonstrated interest in nurturing Christian faith and life), so this page is retained as watchlist intelligence rather than an active opportunity: if Lilly repeats the model (it has repeatedly rolled one-off religion initiatives into successor rounds), a diocesan adult-formation office should be ready to move on the next window.

**What to do next.** Check the award list when announced in December 2026 and watch lillyendowment.org/open-initiatives for any successor round; no application possible in the current cycle.
