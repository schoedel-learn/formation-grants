**What it funds.** Methodist Healthcare Ministries — the 50-50 co-owner of the Methodist Healthcare System
and one of South Texas's largest private health funders — awards roughly $18 million per annual grant
cycle, open competitively across its 74-county South Texas service region (including Bexar and Laredo).
Average grant size is $250,000. Its stated approach is "a holistic approach to health care: mind, body,
and spirit," and requests "must address one or more of the Focus Areas" — Mental & Behavioral Health,
Digital Equity, Housing, Education/Workforce, and Food Security (each with its own Theory of Change).

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan office is a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization operating in Bexar County,
inside the 74-county region; MHM's faith-based mission ("a private, faith-based, not-for-profit
organization") does not restrict applicants, and its Mental & Behavioral Health focus area is the
anchor for diocesan family-wellness, counseling, and family-crisis programming.

**What to do next.** The page documents the 2025 cycle (open-cycle applications were due
October 11, 2024); no 2026 or 2027 open-cycle window is posted on the page as of 2026-10-27. Email
CommInvestments@mhm.org to confirm when the next open competitive cycle opens and whether a diocesan
family-life/counseling request fits the current focus-area strategies before drafting.
