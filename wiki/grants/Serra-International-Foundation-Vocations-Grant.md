**What it funds.** A worldwide competitive grant cycle (about 150 applications a year; $152,150 to 29 organizations in the most recent cycle) funding programs that foster vocations to the priesthood and religious life — seminaries and formation programs, youth evangelization, and campus ministry. Average grant is $3,000; as much as $25,000 has gone to some applicants. General operating expenses, individual tuition/scholarships, primary education for under-tens, and public-welfare organizations are excluded.

**Why we qualify — with a caveat.** The per-lane Serra rule counts this program only if its money can fund faith-formation programming. The evidence is mixed: the recipients page explicitly notes applications are received "to support seminaries and formation programs to youth evangelization and campus ministry," and a past grant funded the Wisconsin Catholic Youth Rally — a worship/faith-formation event for thousands of teens built around vocations promotion. But the grantor's own funding priorities name only "Priestly and Religious Formation; Vocations Outreach; Spiritual Formation of Clergy and Religious" — parish or diocesan faith formation per se is not named. A diocesan vocations office is plainly an "organization" and could route a vocations-anchored faith-formation event through it; because the focus-area mapping is inference rather than verbatim, status is maybe.

**What to do next.** The application form and required question set live at the grant-application page; the online scholarships portal lists the deadline. Confirm with foundation@serrainternational.org whether a diocesan-Office-of-Vocations applicant with a youth faith-formation + vocations event fits before the next September 30 cutoff, and reconcile the two posted deadlines (Sept 30 on the grant page vs "Deadline Oct 01, 2026" on the scholarships portal).
