**What it funds.** CCF Baltimore is the archdiocese's planned-giving engine: donors establish endowments and donor-advised funds, and the foundation distributes those funds to parishes, schools, and ministries of the Archdiocese of Baltimore ($5.3M distributed in 2025 across 450+ funds). Fund recipients named on its fund pages support formation, evangelization, and parish programming — the dollars exist, but they are donor-directed, not competitively applied for.

**Why we qualify (with a caveat).** Our ministries are exactly what distributions support, but there is no open application to fill out. The realistic path for a diocesan office is philanthropic: work with CCF staff and prospective donors to establish or steer a fund toward family-life, marriage, or faith-formation programs.

**What to do next.** Contact CCF (CatholicCommunityFoundation@Archbalt.org, 410-625-8497) to (a) learn whether any existing field-of-interest or parish funds align with formation programming, and (b) brief CCF on our formation priorities so donors establishing new funds can be matched to them. Re-check the site periodically — if CCF ever publishes an open grant cycle, upgrade this page.
