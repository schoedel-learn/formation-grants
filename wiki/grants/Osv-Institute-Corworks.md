**What it funds.** CorWorks (launched October 2026) is OSV Institute's current flagship funding program: a one-week retreat-style accelerator week plus a 90-day implementation roadmap for parish teams, with accepted parishes eligible for up to $25,000 in seed funding from the OSV Institute to launch the parish-owned responses to local, unmet community needs (framed around the Corporal Works of Mercy). This replaces the OSV Institute's earlier Capacity Grant program, whose recipients were announced through 2022.

**Why we qualify.** Parishes (with pastor support) are the applicants, and the diocese's role is precisely what our office controls: endorsing the invitation and circulating CorWorks through bishop/vicar-general and deanery channels. The focus-area caveat: the program funds parish works-of-mercy innovation rather than formation programming as such, so its value to our formation lane depends on the community need a parish proposes to meet — tagged `maybe` accordingly.

**What to do next.** The inaugural parish application window runs October 9–November 20, 2026 — decisions on whether to (a) promote the window to our parishes now (window closes November 20) and (b) pursue diocesan partnership for a future cohort can both start with an email to CORWORKS@OSV.com or a call to 260-355-2238.
