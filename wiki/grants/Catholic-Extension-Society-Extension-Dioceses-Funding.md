**What it funds.** Catholic Extension Society connects under-resourced US dioceses with donor funding and strategic initiatives through six funding categories, two of which are direct matches for our lane: "Building Leaders" (education/training of lay and ordained leaders) and "Engaging Young Catholics" (faith formation for children, teens, young adults, and families). Requests ride on the diocese's annual strategic plan agreed with Extension, not free-form applications.

**Why we qualify.** The applicant can only be the diocese itself — parishes and apostolates apply through the diocesan structure — so a diocesan formation office is exactly the intended applicant, if and only if our diocese is on the approved Extension Dioceses list, hence `maybe`.

**What to do next.** First check whether our diocese appears on the Approved Extension Dioceses list (https://www.catholicextension.org/funding-information/approved-mission-dioceses). If it does, contact our diocese's designated primary contact with Extension (listed on the "How to Make a Request for Funding" page) and route a faith-formation or lay-leader-training request into next year's strategic plan; portal access is on the Funding Information page.
