**What it funds.** Baptist Health Foundation of San Antonio — a major San Antonio institutional
grantmaker (legacy of the Baptist Health System sale) — provides Responsive Grants of up to $300,000 for
mid- to large-sized organizations improving community health. Its Community Investment Grants
($3,000–$12,000) serve small organizations; it also runs invitation-based Strategic-to-Mission grants.
The 2026 Responsive process ran a two-part LOI (Part 1 due Feb 5, 2026; Part 2 due Feb 28, 2026), an
invited application in May, July–August site visits, and trustee decisions in November. The Foundation
explicitly contemplates church applicants — churches are exempt from the revenue-band requirement on
the Community Impact track.

**Why we qualify.** A diocesan office is a 501(c)(3) operating in Bexar County with revenue well above
the $250,000 Responsive floor; the Foundation states it welcomes "a wide variety of organizations" and
no requirement excludes church/diocesan applicants. The fit hinges entirely on the health-relation
requirement: a diocesan counseling, mental-health, or family-wellness program relates to health; a
pure catechesis or OCIA request would not, since "each request must relate to health."

**What to do next.** The 2026 LOI windows have closed ("No longer accepting Letters of Inquiry" on both
tracks as of 2026-10-27). First-time applicants must submit the Grant Inquiries Form
(https://bhfsa.org/connect-with-bhfsa/); email lcantu@bhfsa.org for the projected 2027 LOI window
(the 2026 pattern was Jan 26–Feb 5 for Part 1) before the next cycle.
