**What it funds.** Discrete research and public-engagement projects in six funding areas (plus the 2026–onward "Intelligence Venture"). The relevant door for a diocesan office is Character Virtue Development's "Programs and Applied Research" strand, which supports organizations that "develop, implement, enhance, or assess research-informed character development programs" situated in "a wide variety of youth-serving contexts, including schools, sports, music, media, faith communities, neighborhoods, and/or camps." Awards are large, multi-year project grants negotiated per project (page publishes no ranges).

**Why we don't qualify right now.** The 2026 funding window is closed — the calendar states verbatim: "The 2026 OFI Deadline has now passed. The Foundation is not accepting new requests at this time" (2026 OFI deadline was July 15, 2026) — and "The Foundation will announce its 2027 Cycle Dates in the new year." Note also that JTF "generally fund[s] discrete projects" and its process is "not designed for other types of requests such as donations, operational support, or program support," and each cycle narrows topics (2026 topics were Intelligence-Venture themes such as collective intelligence and character development in formative environments "such as families, schools, workplaces, and digital platforms") — "Projects that focus on topics other than those listed below will not be considered for funding."

**What to do next.** Recheck https://www.templeton.org/grants/grant-calendar in early 2027 when 2027 cycle guidance is issued. If a character/virtue topic that touches family or youth faith formation appears, submit a two-page OFI in the Templeton Portal (portal.templeton.org); the OFI-to-decision path runs roughly 4.5 months for full proposals after an invitation.
