**What it funds.** Up to $20,000 to free a pastoral leader from daily duties for an individual or collaborative study project on Christian life, religious practices, and ministry — output shared through books, podcasts, classes, presentations, and more. The 2027 cycle window is January 1, 2027 – June 30, 2028, with a January 2027 grantee consultation. This is a grant to a named ministry leader (with the project rooted in their diocesan work), not a general office grant.

**Why we qualify.** The Archdiocesan offices for adult faith formation and catechesis employ lay leaders who hold ministerial commissioning from the Archdiocese — exactly the candidate profile the page names ("clergy, lay leaders, and staff working in diverse Christian contexts in the United States and Canada"). A study project on adult faith formation, OCIA catechumenate practice, or family faith transmission fits the program's church-life focus.

**What to do next.** The 2026 cycle closed August 12, 2026; the program is annual, so calendar the 2027 application guide release (watch louisville-institute.org from spring 2027) and pre-identify which formation staff person should carry the project and what release time their supervisor will approve. Contact is via web form or 502-992-5432. Note: the Louisville Institute is Lilly-funded but distinct from Lilly Endowment's direct initiatives already tracked in the index — this is an open application, not an invitation.
