**What it funds.** One of the largest Catholic-friendly grantmakers in Texas, created to support "mostly Catholic educational, charitable, and religious activities primarily in South Texas." Texas is mandatory: all grant funds must be used exclusively within Texas. About 70% of annual sectarian distributions go to Catholic educational, charitable, and religious work in South Texas; the rest to such work elsewhere in Texas. Sectarian grant requests above $50,000 require a three-fourths board vote ("does not favor requests in excess of these amounts"); endowments, debt, and completed projects are not funded. **TEXAS-ELIGIBLE:** the archdiocese is inside the funding state — this funder deserves early attention.

**Why we qualify (partially).** Applicants are OCD-listed Catholic entities anywhere in Texas, so an archdiocesan office or parish-based apostolate can apply — but the page does not break out "faith formation" as a named category, and funding is framed broadly as religious/educational/charitable purposes. The foundation notes applicants must follow diocesan protocols, and the Archdiocese of San Antonio's own grants-training deck (archsa.org, Grants & Giving, Beverly Duke, 2025) lists Kenedy among approved "Catholic friendly funders" for archSA parishes (deadline Oct 15, notifications late May).
