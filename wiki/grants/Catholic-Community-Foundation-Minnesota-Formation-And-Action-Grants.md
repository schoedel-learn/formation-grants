**What it funds.** CCF-MN's discretionary grant programs are aimed squarely at formation in parishes: the Formation and Action program funds learning about Catholic social teaching and putting it into action ("the formation of priests, parish staff, and parishioners," training, speakers, education programs such as JustFaith); Small Group grants fund launching and strengthening parish small-group evangelization ministry; a separate program funds parish youth, young adult, and preschool faith formation, and another funds graduate-level formation for lay ecclesial ministers.

**Why we qualify.** The formation content matches our lane almost perfectly, but the application doorway is the parish: the programs are written for parishes of the Archdiocese of Saint Paul and Minneapolis, and the pages do not say whether a diocesan office may apply directly — hence `maybe`. If our (arch)diocese is in Minnesota, the realistic path is either confirming diocesan-office eligibility with the grants officer or shepherding pilot-parish applications.

**What to do next.** Email Grants Program Officer Bella Eckert (info@ccf-mn.org) to ask (a) whether an (arch)diocesan office is an eligible applicant and (b) the next Formation and Action window — the program page says applications are accepted mid-August to late September each year and the 2026 window has closed ("Check back next year at the end of August").
