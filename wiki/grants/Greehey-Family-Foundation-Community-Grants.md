**What it funds.** The Greehey Family Foundation (Bill and Louree Greehey, San Antonio; two decades of grantmaking) funds Bexar County nonprofits that "strengthen families, uplift youth, care for the elderly, and bring hope to those experiencing homelessness or hardship." Applications run on two annual cycles through Submittable: spring portal January 15–April 1, fall portal July 15–September 30, with Board consideration at each cycle's close and awards of one grant per agency per year (reapplication required).

**Why we qualify — and why this is a maybe.** ArchSA family-life and Catholic Charities programs are 501(c)(3)s serving Bexar County, so the mechanics fit cleanly. The eligibility language, however, never names Catholic or diocesan entities — it is a general community funder — so per our qualification filter this cannot be called eligible outright. The Greeheys are well-known San Antonio Catholic philanthropists, and family strengthening is the Foundation's first named mission area, which is exactly our family-life grant lane. Our grants-received records scan (2025–2027) shows no Greehey grants to ArchSA entities.

**What to do next.** The fall 2026 portal closed September 30; the next window is the spring cycle, portal open January 15, 2027, deadline April 1, 2027. Submit through Submittable with proof of 501(c)(3), program objectives/timeline, detailed project and operations budget, current funders, and board list (full checklist on the guidelines page). The Foundation may request an agency tour — budget lead time.
