**What it funded (2026 guidelines).** The Evangelization Grant supports diocesan-wide, vicariate, parish cluster, and parish programs for Worship, Spirituality, Sacred music, Youth and young adult ministry, Marriage preparation, Family life, Baptism preparation, Campus ministry, Retreat operations, and Activities of the Right of Christian Initiation for Adults (RCIA) programs — a near-exact match to our marriage/OCIA lane. The Lifelong Catechesis Grant funds parish religious education materials and teaching stipends and "the current work of the Diocesan Department of Education inclusive of Adult Faith Formation." Average awards $1,000–$3,000 (Evangelization) and $1,000–$2,500 (Catechesis); cycle opens October 1 and closes December 1, with checks mailed before June 30.

**Why we qualify (partially).** Applicant classes include "other 501 c(3) charitable organizations that align with Catholic teachings" — but all within the Diocese of Green Bay (16-county northeastern Wisconsin). A San Antonio applicant does not serve Green Bay, so practical eligibility fails even though the focus match is the strongest of any community foundation surveyed this round. Speakers and worship performers must be diocese-approved; Bibles, travel, and food are not funded.

**What to do next.** Not actionable from San Antonio. Retain as the model of a community foundation that explicitly names marriage preparation, family life, and RCIA in an evangelization endowment — worth pointing the archSA's development office to when shaping a domestic endowment/grant line.
