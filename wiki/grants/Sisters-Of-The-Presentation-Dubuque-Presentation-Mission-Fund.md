**What it funds.** The Presentation Mission Fund is the Dubuque Presentation Sisters' values-driven grantmaking program with three tracks: Community Grants (one-time/community-strengthening), Ministry Grants (general operating support), and Legacy Grants (transformational/capacity-building). Funding runs on recurring spring and fall cycles guided by core values of dignity, justice, and care for Earth.

**Why we qualify.** Applications are open to any organization, Catholic or not, and the strategic "Education" priority ("Expanding equitable access to quality education, especially for underserved communities") could cover catechetical formation for underserved families — but the priorities do not name religious or faith-catechesis programs, hence `maybe`.

**What to do next.** "Fall grant cycle is closed. Please check back in the spring." Watch dbqpbvms.org/grants for the spring-cycle window, and email PBVMgrants@dbqpbvms.org to ask whether a diocesan faith-formation program qualifies under the Education priority before applying.
