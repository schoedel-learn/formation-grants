**What it funds.** Annual grants (capped at $12,500) from the SSJ Ministry Foundation, established 2003 by the Sisters of Saint Joseph of Rochester, to nonprofits whose purpose is "the alleviation of the conditions which cause ignorance, poverty, and oppression and the fostering of justice and mercy." Recent grantees include the Ignatian Spirituality Project and Notre Dame Retreat House — adult spiritual and retreat ministry — alongside social-service works.

**Why we qualify.** The foundation's own page states it takes applications from ANY 501(c)(3) whose values align with the SSJ mission, and a diocesan office's adult catechesis or retreat-ministry program ("accompany those who search for a deeper union with God") reflects that reconciling mission; the published timeline matches the applicant, so no `maybe` gate applies.

**What to do next.** The 2027 cycle is open NOW — the page says applications are being accepted "by November 1, 2026" (only days away as of the check date). Download and complete the Project Budget Worksheet for upload with the online form; email grants_committee@ssjrochester.org with questions. If the window passes, the cycle repeats each September 1–November 1.
