**What it funds.** The H-E-B Tournament of Champions (golf event, San Antonio) has raised over $194
million since 1986, distributed through the H-E-B Tournament of Champions Charitable Trust "to benefit
Special Olympics Texas and other worthwhile organizations that focus on children and educational
initiatives throughout the State of Texas" — with the stated goal of "improving the lives of Texas
families and supporting educational initiatives." Nonprofits request funding through an online portal
with no posted deadline or amount range on the public site.

**Why we qualify.** A Texas 501(c)(3) whose program improves family life meets the stated frame as
written — the eligibility test on the public site is a statewide, generic-organization one.
