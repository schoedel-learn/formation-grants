**What it funds.** Dollar-for-dollar matching grants to US Catholic dioceses/eparchies to implement
key elements of the USCCB pastoral framework "Called to the Joy of Love: National Pastoral
Framework for Marriage and Family Life Ministry" — marriage preparation, enrichment, and family
ministries balanced across Vision, Skills and Support. Between 2022 and 2024 the fund awarded
$950,000 to 19 dioceses/archdioceses, then set aside $300,000 for the 2025 rounds. Applications
require a proposed project describing fund use, expected outcomes, plus a Diocesan Needs
Assessment and a Diocesan Inventory for Marriage and Family Life Ministry (all downloadable from
the page).

**Why we qualify.** Every stated eligibility condition matches a diocesan Marriage & Family Life
office: US dioceses and eparchies only, a named diocesan applicant, a $10,000–$50,000 matching
award, and our family/marriage focus areas squarely inside the framework's pillars. The catch is
timing, not fit: the most recent posted application rounds closed January 1, 2025 and July 1,
2025, and no 2026 round was on the page at last check.

**What to do next.** Do not write a proposal on the stale dates. Email Dennis Stoica
(Dennis@CatholicMarriageFund.org) to confirm whether a further round is being scheduled, request
the current RFP, and keep the Diocesan Inventory work ready so the office can move quickly when a
round opens. Raising match funds early (local Knights councils, a regional Catholic foundation,
private donors — the pattern successful grantee dioceses used) is the other long lead item.
