**What it funds.** Small enhancement grants — up to $500 — for parish and community events in the areas of "service, family, and faith." Family is named by the grantor verbatim as a grant area, which maps directly onto the office's family-life programming.

**Why we qualify — with a caveat.** "Grants up to $500 are available to parishes and schools through a local Parish Volunteer Team or Council." The money can land on a parish family/faith event, but the application is not made by the parish directly: the conduit is a Catholic United member Parish Volunteer Team or Council — member sponsorship, the standard fraternal pattern. Per lane rules that is a diocesan-easy fix (volunteer teams exist across the five-state membership area), but it is sponsorship-gated, so status stays at maybe rather than eligible.

**What to do next.** The page's own instruction: "To take advantage of this program, contact the Member Engagement staff." Email engage@catholicunited.org (or 1-877-275-7145) asking how a diocesan family-life or faith-formation event pairs with a local Parish Volunteer Team and whether a regional or diocesan-wide event qualifies.
