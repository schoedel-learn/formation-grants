**What it funds.** Year-long projects (up to $25,000) that connect public worship with Christian discipleship and faith formation: liturgical formation, intergenerational worship, children's/youth worship formation, bilingual worship resources, and similar. Awards "range from $8,000 to $25,000" and the program runs as twice-yearly proposal rounds with projects starting in January and July.

**Why we qualify.** Roman Catholic grantees are explicitly part of the program's history — the recipients page states grants have gone "across 40+ denominations and traditions\u2014including Orthodox, Roman Catholic, Pentecostal, non-denominational, and other Protestant communities." ArchSA parishes can apply directly, and the office could carry a diocesan cohort of parish worship-formation projects.

**What to do next.** The October 15, 2026 deadline just passed (projects to begin January 15, 2027 — decision announced in that cycle). The next proposal round is expected in spring 2027 for July-start projects; build a shortlist of 2–4 target parishes (especially bilingual and family-catechesis ones) and watch worship.calvin.edu/grants in Q1 2027. A near-term win could pair OCP Parish Grants (liturgy/music) with this (worship formation).
