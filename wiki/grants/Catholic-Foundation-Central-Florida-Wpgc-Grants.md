**What it funds.** The Orlando diocesan foundation's Women's Philanthropic Giving Circle pools member donations and awards annual grants to "Catholic compliant non-profit organizations" serving the spiritual or corporal needs of people in the Diocese of Orlando's nine counties. Distinct application tracks exist for diocesan parishes/schools/ministries and for non-diocesan nonprofits (the latter with a second-round Pitch Party). Members participate in grant selection.

**Why we qualify (partially).** Non-diocesan nonprofits are explicitly invited, and "spiritual needs" could be read to include faith formation — but the service area is fixed to nine Orlando-area counties, excluding San Antonio-based work. The 2026 deadline (June 1) has passed.

**What to do next.** Not actionable from San Antonio. Watch cfocf.org for the 2027 cycle posting; the foundation also runs stewardship-education grants (ICSC scholarships for Diocese of Orlando parish staff only).
