**What it funds.** Catholic United Financial (the fraternal formerly known as Catholic Financial Life, now a Trusted Fraternal Life brand after the January 1, 2025 merger) runs an always-open online grant portal. The strongest fit is its Chastity and Abstinence Education grant — up to $1,000 to schools or parishes for youth education programs, events, curriculum, or media undertaken "in a collaboration with other parishes, youth groups, schools, regional or diocesan-wide efforts" — i.e., diocesan faith-formation work is explicitly named. Education Technology grants (up to $500 to Catholic schools or parishes) and event sponsorship/matching grants for Catholic-education fundraising round out the menu; the Schuler Fund for seminarians runs Oct 1–31 annually on the same portal.

**Why we qualify.** Parishes, schools, ministries, and diocesan-wide efforts are all named as applicants — only restriction is licensure geography: Minnesota, Iowa, North Dakota, South Dakota, and Wisconsin. A diocesan office in any of those five states applies directly through the online portal. Capped at four figures, but the application is a lightweight portal form, not a cycle race.

**What to do next.** An applicant account at my.catholicunitedfinancial.org is created, "Catholic United Programs" → "Catholic United Grants" in the menu, and a form submitted; questions go to engage@catholicunited.org or 1-877-275-7145. No dates are posted for the rolling grants, so confirm with Member Engagement that the office's project type is welcome before writing.
