**What it funds.** The CCC awards grants once per year for communications projects that use media to spread the Gospel message. Funding comes from the May national diocesan collection: half stays in the diocese for local communications projects, half funds US projects national in scope (plus projects in developing areas of the world). The process is two-stage: a 100-word project summary is screened first; invited applicants submit a full application with budget through the CCC grants portal. CCC is disinclined to fund recurring subscription media, equipment purchases, debt reduction, or multi-year ongoing support (one year at a time).

**Why we qualify (or don't).** A family/faith-formation apostolate's media work (online formation courses, catechetical video or app content aimed at national/regional reach) matches the CCC's mission — but the local-scope exclusion is the deciding ambiguity: the page says verbatim, "Projects that are of an arch/diocesan or more local scope within the United States will *not* be considered by the national CCC; these projects can approach the local arch/diocese for funding consideration. (However, projects that have a realistic potential for wider regional impact or for a particular population within the United States, *may* apply to the national CCC for funding.)" If our project serves one diocese, the money path is the diocese's retained half of the CCC collection, not the national grant.

**What to do next.** Decide the project's scope honestly before spending effort: if it plausibly serves a wider regional audience or a particular US population (e.g., Spanish-language OCIA formation nationally), draft the 100-word summary now and watch for the next portal opening, since the 2026 portal has closed and decisions were due late November 2026. For a local-scope project instead, ask the diocesan communications/how-to-give office about the retained-local-half funds. Contact via the "CCC staff" link on the Grant Information page.
