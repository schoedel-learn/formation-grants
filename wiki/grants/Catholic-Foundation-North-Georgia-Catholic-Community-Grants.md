**What it funds.** The Atlanta archdiocesan community foundation runs two competitive cycles a year (August and February): fall Catholic Community Grants ($2,000–$10,000) for parishes, nonprofits, and Catholic ministries, and spring grants for archdiocesan Catholic schools only ($3,000–$20,000); $1,000 Parish Enrichment Grants for archdiocesan parishes and missions both cycles. Funded purposes are Works of Mercy/community-service work (sanctity of life, elderly care, homelessness, health and mental health).

**Why we do not qualify.** Two independent barriers: (1) applicants must be organizations serving within the boundaries of the Archdiocese of Atlanta, and (2) the funded categories are social-service/Works-of-Mercy programs — marriage/family/faith formation is not a named category. A San Antonio applicant fails both, so `not_eligible` rather than `maybe`.

**What to do next.** No action. Noted as an excluded competitor model: even where a Catholic community foundation accepts nonprofits, it gates on serving its own archdiocese.
