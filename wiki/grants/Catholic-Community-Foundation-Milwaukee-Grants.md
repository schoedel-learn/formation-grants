**What it funds.** An archdiocesan community foundation making limited-term project and program grants (rarely multi-year; no capital, endowment, or emergency grants). The Leadership Development focus area — "forming leaders for parishes, schools and agencies" — and the Community Building focus area — "strengthening families and parishes" — are direct matches for adult/lay formation and family-ministry proposals.

**Why we qualify.** Eligible applicants include organizations listed in the Official Catholic Directory, which covers a diocesan office; the geographic catch is that "the majority of a fund's grants should be directed to the 10 counties of the Archdiocese of Milwaukee," hence `maybe` unless our (arch)diocese is within that footprint.

**What to do next.** Watch the Grant Seekers page for the next fall Education, Lay Leadership and Community cycle: the 2026 cycle's Letter of Intent deadline was August 10, 2026 (awards after the January 2027 board meeting), and the page notes late LOIs "may be considered in next year's fall cycle." Submit the LOI as soon as the next cycle is posted; no next-cycle date was posted as of the check date.
